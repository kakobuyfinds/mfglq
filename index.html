What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds, source links, prices and buying notes. It can help you compare options before purchasing through a shopping agent. A saved listing is a research reference: it does not guarantee stock, quality, authenticity or support by a particular agent.

This page pairs a small, searchable CAFANS catalog selection with a practical comparison guide. Open CAFANS to explore the original product details and available shopping agent options. Confirm whether LoveGoBuy supports your chosen listing before relying on it for an order.

Look beyond the thumbnail

What should you check in QC photos?

Quality control photos help you inspect visible details of the photographed item. They cannot confirm every specification or predict long-term durability.

  • Variant: compare the selected color, size label and model with your order record.
  • Condition: inspect seams, closures, edges and surfaces for visible damage or inconsistencies.
  • Measurements: request a clearly positioned ruler or tape when fit depends on a specific dimension.
  • Contents: confirm whether the listing covers one piece, a pair or a set, and check included accessories.
  • Unanswered questions: request additional photos or clarification while the applicable return window is still open.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official LoveGoBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide with product references from CAFANS. No official LoveGoBuy relationship is claimed. Account, payment and parcel questions belong with the platform handling your order.

Where do the two main buttons take me?

Both “LoveGoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” open https://cafans.com. Product source links open the corresponding CAFANS detail pages. This page has no checkout.

Are these real products from the CAFANS catalog?

The six names, image addresses and estimated USD prices were retrieved from the CAFANS homepage. This is a static selection rather than a live stock feed. Reopen the source listing to confirm current details.

Can every listed item be purchased through LoveGoBuy?

Support has not been verified for these items. Review the product details on CAFANS, obtain the appropriate source link and confirm acceptance with LoveGoBuy before placing an order.

Do the displayed prices include international shipping?

No. They are source catalog item estimates in USD. Domestic delivery, international freight, service fees, currency conversion and destination charges may add to your total.

Do I need Excel or Google Sheets?

No. You can browse this directory directly. A separate spreadsheet is useful if you want to keep personal measurements, comparison notes and order status alongside each source link.

How should I choose a clothing size?

Use the measurement guide for the exact listing. Compare equivalent measurements with clothing you already own, including chest width, length and shoulders where relevant. A familiar letter size alone is not enough.

What if a product link stops working?

Search the product name or ID on CAFANS. A missing page may indicate a changed or removed listing; avoid assuming that a similarly named replacement has the same specifications or price.
